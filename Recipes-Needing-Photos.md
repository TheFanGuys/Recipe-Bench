Dark, moody background, warm side lighting, shallow depth of field, rich realistic textures, appetizing and true to how the dish really looks. Three-quarter angle. **Ingredients for 4 servings**
- 2 tbsp (30 ml) — Olive oil
- 1 — Medium onion, chopped
- 1 — Garlic clove, minced
- 1 tsp (4.9 ml) — Ground cumin
- 1 tsp (4.9 ml) — Pepper
- ½ tsp (2.5 ml) — Chili powder (recipe: ½–1 tsp)
- ½ tsp (2.5 ml) — Salt
- 1 tsp (4.9 ml) — Sriracha or hot pepper sauce (optional)
- 2 — Medium tomatoes, chopped
- 4 — Large eggs
- to taste — Chopped fresh cilantro, for garnish
- to taste — Whole pita breads, toasted, for serving

**Method**
1. Heat the oil in a large heavy skillet over medium heat. Cook the onion, stirring, 4 to 6 minutes until tender.
2. Add the garlic, spices and hot sauce if using; cook 30 seconds more.
3. Add the tomatoes and cook 3 to 5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the mixture thickens.
4. Make a well for each egg with the back of a spoon and crack an egg into each.
5. Cover and cook 4 to 6 minutes, until the whites are completely set and the yolks begin to thicken but aren’t hard.
6. Sprinkle with cilantro; serve with toasted pita.

- to taste — Kosher salt and black pepper

### Homemade Tater Tots

Shown plated and ready to eat on a dark stoneware plate, on a dark rustic wood table. Dark, moody background, warm side lighting, shallow depth of field, rich realistic textures, appetizing and true to how the dish really looks. Three-quarter angle. **Ingredients for 1 batch (2 lb potatoes)**
- 907 g (2 lb) — Russet potatoes, peeled
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) — All-purpose flour
- 1 tsp (4.9 ml) — Garlic powder
- ½ tsp (2.5 ml) — Onion powder
- ¼ tsp (1.2 ml) — Dried oregano
- ¼ tsp (1.2 ml) — Dried dill
- to taste — Kosher salt and black pepper
- 3 cups (710 ml) — Avocado oil, for frying (original: vegetable oil)
- 2 tbsp (30 ml) — Chopped fresh parsley (optional)

**Method**
1. Put the potatoes in a Dutch oven and cover with cold salted water by about 1 inch. Bring to a boil, then lower the heat and simmer, covered, until partly cooked, 6 to 7 minutes. Drain and let cool.
2. Grate the potatoes finely on a box grater. Squeeze in a clean towel or cheesecloth to remove as much water as possible.
3. In a large bowl, mix the potatoes with the flour, garlic powder, onion powder, oregano and dill; season with salt and pepper. The mix should hold together but stay fairly dry. Shape into tots.
4. Heat the oil in a large pot or Dutch oven over medium-high heat to 360°F on a deep-fry thermometer.
5. Fry in batches until golden and crisp, 3 to 4 minutes. Let the oil come back to temperature between batches. Drain on paper towels.
6. Serve right away, with parsley if you like.
