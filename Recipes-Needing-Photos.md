### Chicken & Quinoa Bowls

**Ingredients for 4 servings**
- 1 cup (237 ml) — Quinoa, rinsed
- 1½ cups (355 ml) — Water
- 680 g (1½ lb) — Chicken tenders
- 2 tsp (9.9 ml) — Paprika
- 1½ tsp (7.4 ml) — Ground coriander
- 1 tsp (4.9 ml) — Onion powder
- ½ tsp (2.5 ml) — Turmeric
- ¾ tsp (3.7 ml) — Salt
- ½ tsp (2.5 ml) — Pepper
- 2 tbsp (30 ml) — Olive oil
- 6 cups (1.42 L) — Chopped kale
- 284 g (10 oz) — Cherry tomatoes (1 pint)
- ½ cup (118 ml) — Slivered almonds
- to taste — Chopped parsley, for garnish

**Method**
1. Simmer the quinoa in the water, covered, until absorbed, about 15 minutes. Fluff.
2. Mix the spices and rub over the chicken.
3. Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Cook the chicken until it reaches 165°F, 3–4 minutes per side.
4. Divide the quinoa, kale, tomatoes, chicken and almonds among 4 containers. Top with parsley.

### Hunan Beef
**File name:** `hunan-beef.jpg`

**Image prompt:**
> Professional food photograph of Hunan Beef, made with beef, flank steak, oyster sauce, peppers & aromatics, red holland pepper, green bell pepper or poblano, red bell pepper. Shown plated and ready to eat on a dark stoneware plate, on a dark rustic wood table. Dark, moody background, warm side lighting, shallow depth of field, rich realistic textures, appetizing and true to how the dish really looks. Three-quarter angle. No text, no labels, no hands, no logos. Square image.

**Ingredients for 4 servings**
- Beef
- 454 g (1 lb) — Flank steak, sliced 1/4" thick
- 1.2 g (1¼ tsp) — Baking soda
- 30 g (2 tbsp) — Water
- 5.0 g (1 tsp) — Oyster sauce
- 43 g (4¾ tbsp) — Cornstarch (for dredging)
- Peppers & aromatics
- 25 g (1 oz) — Red Holland pepper, deseeded (or red Fresno)
- 100 g (3½ oz) — Green bell pepper or poblano, deseeded
- 100 g (3½ oz) — Red bell pepper, deseeded
- 80 g (3 oz) — Banana peppers, deseeded
- 2.0 g (0 oz) — Whole dried chili peppers
- 8.0 g (2¾ tbsp) — Fresh ginger, sliced
- 40 g (1½ oz) — Shallots, thinly sliced
- 15 g (1¾ tbsp) — Garlic, sliced
- 20 g (½ oz) — Fermented black beans, rinsed (Chinese grocery)
- 60 g (2 oz) — Scallions, cut into 2" pieces
- Sauce
- 2.0 g (½ tsp) — Granulated sugar
- 30 g (2 tbsp) — Hot water
- 2.5 g (½ tsp) — Chinese black vinegar
- 38 g (2½ tbsp) — Soy sauce
- 0.50 g (½ tsp) — White pepper
- Rest of the dish
- 73 g (5 tbsp) — Vegetable or canola oil (for frying)
- 30 g (2 tbsp) — Shaoxing wine

**Method**
1. Combine the beef with the baking soda, water, and oyster sauce; massage until absorbed. Marinate 30 minutes, then dredge in cornstarch.
2. Slice the fresh peppers; leave the dried chilies whole unless you want it spicier.
3. Stir the sugar into the hot water, then add the vinegar, soy sauce, and white pepper. Set aside.
4. Heat the wok until smoking. Sear the beef in 3 batches in the oil until browned and crisp. Set aside, leaving about 2 tbsp oil in the wok.
5. Add the ginger; fry 30 seconds until caramelized. Add the shallots; fry 30 seconds. Add the fresh peppers; stir-fry 1 minute on high heat.
6. Push aside and toast the dried chilies 20 seconds. Add the Shaoxing wine around the pan, then the garlic, black beans, and red pepper. Stir-fry 30 to 60 seconds.
7. Add the fried beef and the sauce. Stir-fry on high heat until most of the sauce has evaporated — this dish is meant to be dry, not saucy. Add the scallions and serve.

Source: https://thewoksoflife.com/hunan-beef/

- 50 g (1 egg) — Large egg
- 7.5 g (1¼ tsp) — Salt
- 0.30 g (⅛ tsp) — Black pepper, a pinch
- 14 g (1 tbsp) — Oil, divided, plus more for frying
- 130 g (1 cup) — Cornstarch, plus 1 tbsp

- Sauce
- 14 g (4¾ tbsp) — Fresh ginger root, minced

### Ted’s Montana Grill Pickled Cucumbers
**File name:** `teds-montana-grill-pickled-cucumbers.jpg`

**Image prompt:**
> Professional food photograph of Ted’s Montana Grill Pickled Cucumbers, made with cucumber, white vinegar, granulated sugar, dill. Shown plated and ready to eat on a dark stoneware plate, on a dark rustic wood table. Dark, moody background, warm side lighting, shallow depth of field, rich realistic textures, appetizing and true to how the dish really looks. Three-quarter angle. No text, no labels, no hands, no logos. Square image.

**Ingredients for 4 servings**
- 1 — Large cucumber, cut in ¼-inch slices
- 1 cup (237 ml) — Water
- ½ cup (118 ml) — White vinegar
- ¼ cup (59 ml) — Granulated sugar
- 1 tsp (4.9 ml) — Salt
- ¼ tsp (1.2 ml) — Black pepper
- ¼ cup (59 ml) — Fresh dill, chopped

**Method**
1. Slice the cucumber into even ¼-inch rounds with a mandoline or sharp knife. Thinner slices go mushy.
2. Whisk the water, vinegar, sugar, salt and pepper 2–3 minutes until the sugar and salt dissolve. Warm water speeds it up.
3. Pack the cucumber and dill into a glass jar, leaving ½ inch at the top. Pour in the brine so everything is covered.
4. Let the jar sit at room temperature at least 2 hours. Much longer and they get too sour.
5. Refrigerate at least 30 minutes and serve cold.
